OBJECTIVE:
    The program takes in a bunch of names and pairs them in a random manner.

LIMITATIONS:
    The program doesn't take player's proficiency at badminton into account. (for now)

ALGORITHM:
1. Initially, random numbers are assigned to each player.
    - from 1 to total number of players.
    - initial focus is upto 12 players for two courts.
2. Players shouldn't be paired together more than twice in a row.
3. Players shouldn't be benched twice in a row.
    - it should effectively cycle all players to be benched at least once before repeating.
4. Player count:
    - 5 (preferably one court)
        - 1 player benched
    - 6 (preferably one court)
        - pair and play against all teams before shuffle.
        - winners play two games in a row, at most.
        - a total of 3 games before re-pairing.
    - 7 (preferably one court)
        - 1 gets to pair with 2 and 7 in round 1 and round 2 of games, respectively.
    - 8 (preferably two courts)
        - pair and play against all teams before shuffle.
        - a total of 6 games before re-pairing.
    - 9 to 11 (preferably two courts)
        - 1, 2, and 3 players are benched respectively.
    - 12 (preferably two courts)
        - 6 pairs, similar to a 6-player shuffle.
        - the pairs are separated into two brackets.
        - play against all of them in that bracket.
        - teams are shuffled, not the pairs.
        - a total of 15 games before re-pairing.

3. Pairs generated are returned as dict() to help with JSON conversions.

4. Will be switching to MongoDB, a non-relational db.
    - it is necessary to maintain player_list, benched_player, and lucky_player as list[str]
    - it isn't supported in SQLAlchemy, in a straight forward way.
    - I tried using MutableList, but that brought its own set of challenges.
    - I could try using 1dim-array in PostgreSQL.
    - In any case, I do not require any relational mapping, ACID transactions or complex joins in my db. So, switching to MongoDB seems wise.

5. Split the functionality into two routes
    - pair router "/pair"
        - "/register" -> user registers his players before they can be paired.
        - "/shuffle" -> pairs the players in random manner.
    - user router "/user"
        - "/list" -> lists the players registered under the user.
        - "/remove_player" -> removes a player from the list of registered players under the user.
        - "/add_player" -> adds a player from the list of registered players under the user.
        - "/update" -> updates the players registered under the user. (multiple add / removes)
        - "/delete" -> deletes the user session.
